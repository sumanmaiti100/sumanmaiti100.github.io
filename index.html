About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, advised by Prof. Soumyajit Dey. My research is on the security of cyber-physical systems, with a focus on smart grids. I work at the intersection of reinforcement learning and formal methods to analyse vulnerabilities in grid operation and to design verifiable, safe defences.

I received my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from Kalyani Government Engineering College in 2021. I am a recipient of the CHANAKYA PhD Fellowship from the AI4ICPS Hub, IIT Kharagpur.

Research

Modern power grids depend on networked sensing, communication and control, which exposes them to coordinated cyber-physical attacks. My research develops methods to identify such vulnerabilities systematically and to defend against them with formal guarantees. I combine reinforcement learning with formal methods (Metric Temporal Logic specifications, SMT solving and falsification) in three directions:

  1. Attack synthesis and vulnerability analysis

    Learning-based and falsification-guided synthesis of targeted attacks on power generation and distribution systems and on electricity markets. [CCS '23, SPACE '26, TCPS, under review]

  2. Verification of learning-enabled controllers

    Specifying time-bounded safety and recovery requirements in Metric Temporal Logic and checking learned controllers against them with SMT-based verification. [IECON '26]

  3. Safe and resilient control

    Safe reinforcement learning for attack mitigation, game-theoretic adaptive protection, and privacy-preserving real-time pricing. [IECON '26, IECON '26, TCPS '25]

Keywords: cyber-physical systems security, smart grids, formal methods, Metric Temporal Logic, safe reinforcement learning, SCADA / ICS protocols (DNP3, Modbus).
